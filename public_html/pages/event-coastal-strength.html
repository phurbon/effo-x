Three days on Miami's waterfront with sunrise recovery, sunset lifts, and floating runways that show how Effo-X shapes move in humidity. Built alongside Tidal Collective and a roster of coastal coaches.

Festival pillars

The festival is staged on floating docks and shaded decks, so every moment keeps water, wind, and movement in view. Effo-X brings breathable textiles, cooling accessories, and stylists on site.

  • Waterproof demo bay to test quick-dry fabrics
  • Cooling bar with ice towels and electrolyte tonics
  • Panel on inclusive coastal training spaces

Programming highlights

Morning Open-water mobility

Guided stretch & swim sessions led by Effo-X ambassadors with waterproof audio.

Afternoon Product concierge appointments

Personal fittings with heat-mapping feedback and embroidery for partner gyms.

Evening Sunset lifts & runway

Live DJ, stacked strength circuits, and a floating catwalk for the new tropical capsule.

How partners activate

Host a takeover class, bring your athletes for fittings, or co-produce content from the dockside studio. We supply assets, signage, and PRO pricing to resell on site.

  • Private lockers for affiliate crews
  • Wholesale bundles delivered before arrival
  • Full social toolkit in English and Spanish
