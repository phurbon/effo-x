What these technologies do

Cookies, local storage, session storage and similar technologies remember carts and sessions, authenticate users, secure transactions, save choices and—only where permitted—measure or personalize use.

Managing consent

Nonessential identifying, profiling, geolocation, analytics or advertising technologies must remain off until you choose to activate them. You may change your choice through “Cookie settings” in the footer. Browser controls can also remove cookies, but disabling necessary storage may break carts or login.
