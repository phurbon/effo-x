## Status

## Requested reviewer qualifications

Québec lawyer experienced in the Consumer Protection Act distance-sale provisions, the Charter of the French Language, Québec private-sector privacy law/Law 25, PIPEDA, CASL, e-commerce, advertising and intellectual property.

## Materials to review

- All files under `pages/privacy.html`, `pages/cookies.html`, `pages/terms.html`, `pages/shipping.html`, `pages/returns.html` and `pages/fr/`.
- Live English and French storefronts, products, cart, account registration, newsletter and custom-apparel form.
- Shopify policies, checkout, confirmation page, confirmation email and refund flow.
- `shopify-quebec-checkout-audit.md` and controlled test-order evidence.
- Data map, vendor agreements, retention schedule, incident-response plan and privacy impact assessments.
- `media-rights-register.md` and supporting evidence.

## Questions for counsel

1. Are all mandatory Québec distance-contract disclosures presented clearly before payment and reproduced in the durable contract?
2. Are French terms, checkout, product information and communications available on equally favourable terms, including the standard-form contract sequence?
3. Do the privacy notice, consent mechanisms, cross-border assessments, incident process and governance disclosures satisfy Québec law and PIPEDA?
4. Does newsletter consent and recordkeeping satisfy CASL?
5. Are return exclusions, delivery language, disclaimers, limitation clauses and custom-apparel terms enforceable?
6. Are product, advertising, sustainability/performance claims substantiated?
7. Are image, model, partner-logo, product-design and customer-supplied artwork rights sufficient?
